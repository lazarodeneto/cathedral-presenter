# Cinematic template backgrounds

The twelve water, dove, Calvary, prayer, worship and night templates use photographic backgrounds under stable, allowlisted URLs in `public/media/cinematic/`. The old silhouette PNGs and their inline base64 copies were removed from the defaults.

Only the camera framing moves: a 48-second eased alternate pan/zoom, with fixed opacity and static contrast shading. There is no shimmer, animated brightness, pulsing light, twinkling or particle layer in these scenes. Text remains stationary. Thumbnails freeze the camera. Reduced-motion preferences freeze the camera and pause the dove video; videos also pause outside the viewport and when the document is hidden.

The dove uses the user's supplied video, with its black background blended into the photographic sky. The other five scenes are photographs with gentle camera motion, not newly generated live-action video. The supplied video and its poster are bundled for offline playback.

## Asset provenance and prompts

Six photographic stills were generated with the built-in image generation tool. Generated originals remain in the Codex generated-images directory; JPEG delivery assets live in this repository. The worship photograph was then edited with the same built-in tool to show two raised open hands with relaxed, bent elbows on each worshipper who raises their hands. The first single-arm version is not shipped.

Common direction: full-bleed 16:9 photorealistic cinema photography, realistic anatomy and physical lighting, restrained grading, dark negative space for white lyrics, no text, logos, drawings or particles.

- `water.jpg`: a tranquil natural lake at blue hour before sunrise; small real ripples with muted gold reflections; softly focused distant mountains and atmospheric mist; slate blue and dark teal; a low camera close to the water.
- `calvary.jpg`: three simple rough wooden empty crosses on a distant rocky hill near Jerusalem at dusk; layered real clouds, muted amber sunset, charcoal earth and subtle atmospheric haze; crosses modestly placed in the lower third; no people, blood, graphic rays or starbursts.
- `prayer.jpg`: a real adult kneeling quietly in a dim stone church, seen from behind in the lower right; anatomically correct hands and posture; soft cool morning window light, realistic masonry and fabric; calm, dark left and center areas for lyrics.
- `worship-v3.jpg`: an authentic diverse church congregation from behind in a dark auditorium, with restrained out-of-focus amber and indigo stage light; people in the lower third. Every person who raises their hands shows both open palms with relaxed bent elbows in a balanced U-shaped worship posture. No single raised straight arm, salute or military gesture. Five fingers per hand, no duplicate or fused limbs. Personal appearance, wide composition and lighting were preserved during the edit. `worship.jpg` and `worship-v2.jpg` are identical compatibility copies for previously saved slides.
- `night.jpg`: a realistic starry night over a dark mountain lake; a delicate Milky Way, sparse natural stars, a small crescent moon and quiet silver reflections; deep midnight blue and natural scale; no drawn hills or glitter.
- `light.jpg`: a quiet dawn sky above distant dark hills, layered ivory and slate clouds with natural gold light; physically plausible atmospheric scattering and dark lower-middle negative space for lyrics; no bird baked into the image, decorative rays or fire graphics.

`dove.mp4` and `dove-poster.jpg` come from the video supplied earlier by the user. They were not generated by the image tool.
